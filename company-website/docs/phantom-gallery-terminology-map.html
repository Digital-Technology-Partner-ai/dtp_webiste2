The plan cards are now drawn as angular wedges. If card visual width is larger than slot spacing, the wedges overlap, matching the kind of crowding you see in the live sphere. Radius changes perceived spaciousness, while FOV changes camera pressure.
ACTIVE ARCThe angular field where cards and belt buttons are distributed.
QUIET ZONEThe intentionally empty rear sector behind the user.
YAWHorizontal rotation around the sphere, like turning left or right.
PITCHVertical tilt, like looking up toward the upper row or down toward the lower row.
EQUATORThe central navigation belt around the sphere.
BANDSThe upper and lower rows of destination cards.
RADIUSThe distance from the user at the center to the gallery surface.
