Signal Cube
Depth Map

A tuning board for describing how the floating green cubes enter the forward view. It separates entry angle, entry spread, card boundary, front depth, rear depth, fade strength, randomness and repetition.

NOT SITE CODE
FRONT DEPTH 9
REAR DEPTH 21
FADED OPACITY 19%
REPETITION medium
Card Boundary means the visible green outline of the card. A rear-depth cube should fade when it crosses into that outline and return to normal when it crosses back out. Cubes should continue travelling until they leave the active card field and reach the empty zone before restarting.
SIGNAL CUBESThe total number of green cubes active in the forward field.
ENTRY SPREADThe angular range where cubes can appear. A wider spread lets cubes enter from more side angles.
DEPTH TRAVELHow far cubes move through depth as they enter, cross and leave the scene.
MOTION SPEEDHow quickly cubes drift across the field before restarting in the empty zone.
REAR DEPTH RATIOThe percentage of cubes assigned to behind-card behavior. The rest are counted as Front Depth.
REAR FADE STRENGTHHow strongly rear-depth cubes fade when crossing a card boundary.
CARD BOUNDARY PADDINGOptional extra margin outside the visible green card outline. At 0 degrees, fading starts exactly at the card boundary.
RANDOMNESSHow irregular cube placement, spacing and movement feel. Higher randomness reduces obvious repetition.
ENTRY PHASEMoves the timing/starting point of the cube pattern through its loop.
CARD BOUNDARY WIDTHThe visible green card outline width in plan view. This represents the card edge the cube crosses.
