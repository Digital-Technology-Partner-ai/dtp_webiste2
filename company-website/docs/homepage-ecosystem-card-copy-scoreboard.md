# Homepage Ecosystem Card Copy Scoreboard

This document records the copy process used for the large 3D ecosystem cards on the homepage. The equator belt labels stay navigational. The main cards carry the visitor-facing hook.

Scores are out of 10.

Judges:

- Skeptical CFO: risk, cost, credibility, ROI and vague claims.
- Distracted founder: two-second clarity and emotional pull.
- Competitor: differentiation and whether the line is easy to copy.
- Ideal customer: fit to the real leadership situation.
- Conversation copywriter: natural rhythm, specificity and persuasion.
